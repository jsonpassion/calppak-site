1. The service

Calppak (캘빡 in Korean, the "app") is a Mac menu bar app and an iPhone and iPad app provided by ForgeLab. On your device, it finds events with dates in text or screenshots you copy, shows them to you, and adds the ones you check and choose to the calendar you pick, or saves them as an .ics file. It works entirely on your device, with no account or server.
